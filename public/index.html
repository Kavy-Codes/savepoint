FAQ

Good to know

Does this download the whole video?

No. yt-dlp resolves direct stream URLs, then ffmpeg seeks to your start time and reads only your range. A 30-second clip moves a few megabytes, not gigabytes.

What do the quality options mean?

Video + Audio merges the best video and audio streams at that resolution into an MP4. Video only is silent (handy for B-roll). MP3 and M4A switch the menu to audio bitrates.

Is there a time limit?

Recommended max is 30 minutes — stream URLs expire on longer clips. Shorter ranges are more reliable.

Which format should I pick?

MP4 for editing and sharing, MP3 for universal audio, M4A for smaller AAC audio at higher quality. Downloads land in your browser's normal downloads bar.
